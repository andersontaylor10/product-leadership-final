# Team Charter: Meridian Foundations

> Module 3 · Set the Habits That Make a Team Faster

## 1. What We Own

> **We own:** The Foundations field-capture experience: the 4-field daily log, offline save-and-sync, and in-app photo markup, owned by PM1 with mobile engineering and UX research and measured on KR1 (weekly field entries) and KR2 (office corrections). The mobile engineers committed to these Now rocks are ring-fenced for the quarter.
>
> **Out of scope:** Enterprise analytics, executive dashboards, ERP integrations and top-10 account requests belong to Enterprise (PM2), including the data model and Procore fixes in Next. Renewal-driven asks from Enterprise sales go into PM2's backlog, never directly to field engineers; the mid-size rollout belongs to CS after the churn interviews.
>
> **Joint calls:** PM1 and PM2 must both sign off before anyone moves mobile engineering off a Now rock or changes a field-captured data point that finance or compliance reports depend on (such as dropping a daily-log field). Data-model schema decisions that change how field data is captured are also joint.

## 2. How We Decide

> **Who decides scope:** PM1 makes feature and scope calls inside field adoption and mobile, and PM2 makes them inside Enterprise; neither can commit the other's engineers. The engineering lead decides how and when work is built inside the scope the PM sets.
>
> **How cross-team conflicts escalate:** If PM1 and PM2 can't agree within 2 business days, either one sends the Head of Product a short written case (the ask, the KR it puts at risk, the engineering cost), and the Head of Product decides within 3 business days. Now rocks hold by default; the only exception is a named renewal above an agreed ARR threshold (e.g., $500K) closing within 60 days, and any trade is announced to both teams and Enterprise sales.
